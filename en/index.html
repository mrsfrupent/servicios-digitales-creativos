01 · About me

A curious mind with lots of tools.

I spend my life asking “so why isn't this working?”. It doesn't matter if it's a club's Instagram, the layout of an exhibition, a company's internal process or the technique of a clean.

I don't box myself into a single discipline, because problems don't either. I come from the creative world, I love technology and I train strength. That mix is exactly what lets me see solutions that are invisible from inside a single field.

My way of working is always the same: listen, understand, find the flaw and build something that fixes it. No fluff, no templates and lots of colour.

02 · How I think

Everything is a system that can be improved.

A brand, an app, an exhibition and a squat have something in common: if something fails, you can find out why. This is my method, always the same.

  1. 1

    I observe

    I take a calm look: your business, your audience, your space or your movement. I ask a lot.

  2. 2

    I find the problem

    The real one, not the symptom. Sometimes “I have no followers” means “I don't know what to say”.

  3. 3

    I design the solution

    I choose the right tool: code, image, words, space or a training programme.

  4. 4

    I test and adjust

    Measure, correct, repeat. Just like weightlifting: technique gets sharper with every attempt.
